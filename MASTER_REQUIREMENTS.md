# Master Requirements — Dravyaguna World

## Mission
Build an independent, world-class plant information and learning platform specifically for BAMS/Dravyaguna study. This project must remain independent from DravyaGuna-97 so the two can be compared fairly.

## Core users
- BAMS undergraduate students
- Dravyaguna learners and teachers
- Students preparing for revision, viva, MCQs, and practical plant identification

## Core product
1. Plant catalog and discovery
2. Fast search by botanical, Sanskrit, common names, synonyms and safe transliteration variants
3. Structured plant dossier
4. Taxonomy and identity
5. Morphology and distinguishing features
6. Habitat/distribution
7. Useful plant parts
8. Rasa, Guna, Virya, Vipaka, Karma and Dosha fields where academically supported
9. Therapeutic uses/indications with source attribution
10. Classical formulations and references
11. Plant-part image gallery with identity/part/source/license/alt-text metadata
12. Supplementary plant records where appropriate
13. Plant comparison
14. MCQ, quiz, viva and flashcard learning
15. Favorites, recent views, notes/progress where appropriate
16. Feedback and issue reporting
17. SEO-friendly canonical plant pages
18. Responsive PWA/mobile experience
19. Accessibility, performance and security
20. Admin/data-quality workflows
21. Automated validation, testing and image/data audits

## Academic integrity
- Never fabricate references, shlokas, properties, indications, taxonomy or images.
- Every high-value academic claim must be traceable to a source or explicitly marked unverified.
- Distinguish classical Ayurvedic claims from modern scientific evidence.
- Preserve Sanskrit terminology accurately.
- Botanical identity must be treated as a verification problem, not inferred from filenames alone.

## Quality gates
No feature is complete until it has appropriate automated tests, data validation, accessibility/performance checks, and review evidence.

## Scope guard
Do not add unrelated BAMS subjects. Plant study is the product boundary.
