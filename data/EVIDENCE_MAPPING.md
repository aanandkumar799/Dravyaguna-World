# Evidence mapping workflow

Each plant has seven evidence targets: identity, taxonomy, morphology, Dravyaguna properties, useful parts, therapeutic uses, and formulations.

Priority source categories:
- **Taxonomy:** expert-reviewed taxonomic authority such as Kew POWO/WCVP. POWO provides taxonomy, identification, distribution, traits and other plant information. citeturn0search1turn0search6
- **Classical Ayurveda:** primary classical texts and appropriate critical editions/translations. CCRAS states that its literary-research work includes critical editions of major classical treatises and maintains the APTA Digital Library. citeturn0search3
- **Pharmacopoeial/formulary:** API/AFI and other official sources where applicable. CCRAS documents API as pharmacopoeial standards and AFI as a collection of classical Ayurvedic formulations. citeturn0search27turn0search28
- **Modern evidence:** peer-reviewed research or official research sources, kept distinct from classical claims.

No claim is upgraded to **verified** merely because a source exists. The reviewer must check that the source actually supports the specific claim and record an appropriate locator.

## Current matrix
The code generates seven evidence tasks for every canonical plant. This is a workflow scaffold; it is not itself evidence and must not be presented to students as verification.
